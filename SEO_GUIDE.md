## 📊 Next Steps: Google Search Console

### Step 1: Add Your Site to Google Search Console

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Choose "URL prefix" and enter: `https://gamevaultdeals.com`
4. Click "Continue"

### Step 2: Verify Ownership

**Method 1: HTML File Upload (Easiest)**
1. Google will give you an HTML file to download
2. Send me the file and I'll add it to your site root
3. Click "Verify" in Search Console

**Method 2: DNS Verification (Alternative)**
1. Google will give you a TXT record
2. Add it to your Strato DNS settings
3. Click "Verify"

### Step 3: Submit Your Sitemap

Once verified:
1. Go to "Sitemaps" in the left sidebar
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"

Google will now crawl and index your site!

### Step 4: Monitor Performance

After 24-48 hours, you'll see:
- Search queries that show your site
- Click-through rates
- Average position in search results
- Indexing status
- Mobile usability issues
